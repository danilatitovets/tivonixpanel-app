# Cost of Normal — Episode 03 Shot Map

Canonical script: `episode-03-script-final.md`
Target: 7–10 min premium documentary; >=20 distinct real/evidence visual sources; typical shot length 2.5–6s.

## Visual grammar
- Real footage dominates; evidence scenes punctuate claims.
- No AI presenter, generic finance icons, fake policy documents, permanent captions or meme typography.
- 4K preferred; sharp native 1080p accepted. Never stretch visibly soft stock to simulate 4K.
- Evidence cards: Inter, restrained off-white text, one accent, source/date visible but secondary.

## Sequence

### 01 — Renewal shock / hook
Narration: renewal higher; no crash/ticket/new teen.
Visuals: real driver opening insurance renewal on laptop/phone; parked car; clean driving POV; close-up reaction without melodrama.
Need: 3–4 distinct sources.
Evidence: none.

### 02 — Price of future risk
Narration: premium is not merely a personal-driving score.
Visuals: traffic flow / varied vehicles / insurer or actuarial document detail / map-like real city aerial.
Need: 2–3 sources.
Graphic: minimal `YOUR RECORD ≠ THE WHOLE PRICE`.

### 03 — Modern repair complexity
Narration: bumper + cameras/radar/sensors/calibration.
Visuals: modern bumper/sensor closeups; technician calibration; collision repair shop; bodywork.
Need: 4–5 sources.
Evidence scene: AAA 2023 study; on-screen `UP TO 37.6%` with explicit `3 tested 2023 vehicles / specific scenarios` qualifier.

### 04 — Repair inflation
Narration: August 2026 maintenance/repair CPI.
Visuals: mechanic labor, parts counter, invoice/calculator, vehicle on lift.
Need: 3–4 sources.
Evidence scene: BLS August 2026 — `MAINTENANCE + REPAIR +5.2% YoY`; secondary `SERVICING +7.9%` / `REPAIR +2.6%`.

### 05 — Injury severity
Narration: liability, medical/legal/settlement severity; IRC historical data.
Visuals: non-graphic accident aftermath; ambulance/ER exterior; legal paperwork/courthouse exterior; insurance claim workflow.
Need: 3–4 sources.
Evidence scene: `~$14K → >$20K` average BI claim payment, `2017 → 2022`, clearly labeled historical IRC study.

### 06 — ZIP code / risk pooling
Narration: location/model/local experience.
Visuals: suburban parking, dense city traffic, cars parked overnight, contrasting neighborhoods/roads.
Need: 3–4 sources.
Graphic: clean chain `DRIVER / VEHICLE / LOCATION / POOL`.

### 07 — Theft nuance
Narration: national theft fell sharply; local/model exposure differs.
Visuals: parked cars at night, anti-theft/locked vehicle, city parking; avoid sensational staged theft footage unless clearly generic B-roll.
Need: 2–3 sources.
Evidence scene: NICB `659,880` / `−23.2%` / `2025`; explicit `national total` label.

### 08 — Weather without driver fault
Narration: hail/flood/wildfire/storm comprehensive losses.
Visuals: hail on vehicles, flooded street with cars, storm-damaged parked cars; publication-safe real footage.
Need: 3–4 sources.
No unsupported national dollar graphic.

### 09 — Rate filings / lag
Narration: state regulation, filings, SERFF, claims first → rates later.
Visuals: real state DOI/SERFF page capture or official filing PDF; actuarial/rate-table detail; calendar/time passage.
Need: 1–2 official evidence assets + 1 real transition source.
Graphic: `LOSSES → FILING/RATE CHANGE → RENEWAL` with state-rules caveat.

### 10 — 2025 industry turn
Narration: NAIC H1 2025 liability DPW +6.1%, physical damage +3.6%; improved underwriting/capacity/competition.
Visuals: NAIC report screenshot + neutral highway/insurer-office B-roll.
Need: 1 official document + 1–2 real sources.
Evidence scene must say `DIRECT WRITTEN PREMIUMS`, not `average policy rate`.

### 11 — 2026 reversal / central reveal
Narration: insurance CPI −5.1% while maintenance/repair +5.2%.
Visuals: clean full-frame evidence graphic with subtle moving real-car background; repair shop intercut.
Need: existing/new repair footage.
Hero graphic: two-column `INSURANCE −5.1%` vs `MAINTENANCE + REPAIR +5.2%`, `Aug 2026 vs Aug 2025 — BLS`.

### 12 — National average ≠ your renewal
Narration: carrier/state/vehicle/coverage can diverge.
Visuals: renewal document close-up, different cars/geographies, driver comparing quotes.
Need: 2–3 sources.
Graphic: `NATIONAL CPI ≠ YOUR RENEWAL`.

### 13 — Nuance / consumer action
Narration: not every increase justified; compare identical coverage; insurers/regulators have agency.
Visuals: quote comparison on laptop, policy coverage detail, regulator filing/document.
Need: 2–3 sources.
Avoid promising savings.

### 14 — Closing synthesis
Narration: history/car/repair/injury/location/pool; uncertain future losses priced today.
Visuals: rapid but restrained callback montage from distinct earlier sources; final parked/driving wide shot.
Need: no new sources required if callbacks remain unobtrusive.
Graphic: none until final line; optional minimal `THE NUMBER IS THE OUTPUT.`

## Acquisition gate
Target library before edit: >=24 distinct usable sources to provide margin above the >=20 final-source floor.
Category minimums: hook/consumer 3; repair/ADAS 5; road/vehicle 4; injury/legal 3; geography/theft 3; weather 3; official evidence assets 4 (AAA/BLS/NICB/NAIC + rate filing where useful).
Do not proceed to final edit if a major category is represented by a single repeatedly reused clip.
