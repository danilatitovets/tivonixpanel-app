## Quantitative claims cleared

### August 2026 CPI — PASS
Primary source: U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics, CPI-U Table 2, August 2026.
- Motor vehicle maintenance and repair: +5.2% YoY.
- Motor vehicle maintenance and servicing: +7.9% YoY.
- Motor vehicle repair: +2.6% YoY.
- Motor vehicle insurance: -5.1% YoY; -0.8% seasonally adjusted MoM.
Editorial constraint: CPI is an aggregate consumer-price index, not a claim-cost index and not a prediction of any individual renewal.

### 2025 H1 auto premiums / underwriting — PASS WITH WORDING GUARDRAIL
Primary source: NAIC, U.S. Property & Casualty and Title Insurance Industries — 2025 First Half Results.
- Private passenger auto liability direct premiums written: +6.1% YoY.
- Auto physical damage DPW: +3.6% YoY.
- NAIC says improved underwriting results and policyholder surplus increased capacity and competition and likely drove insurers to pull back from large recent rate increases.
- Auto liability PDLR worsened 6.2 points to 67.3% amid an 18.8% increase in direct losses incurred; NAIC explicitly notes increased claims severity.
Editorial constraint: DPW growth is not identical to an average policyholder rate increase.

### Vehicle theft — PASS
Primary source: NICB, March 18 2026 release and July 15 2026 update.
- 659,880 vehicles reported stolen in 2025.
- Down 23.2% from 850,708 in 2024.
- Lowest level in several decades.
- NICB explicitly says location/theft risk can be part of underwriting.
Editorial constraint: do not claim a current nationwide theft surge; retain local/model-specific risk framing.

### ADAS repair complexity — PASS, DATED
Primary source: AAA, December 14 2023 ADAS repair study.
- AAA found ADAS components could account for up to 37.6% of total repair cost in its tested crash scenarios.
- Individual ADAS component repair/calibration costs in the study ranged roughly $290–$1,596, above normal bodywork.
Editorial constraint: study used three 2023 vehicles and specific scenarios. Use as evidence that modern sensing/calibration can add repair complexity/cost, not as a universal 2026 price quote.

### Bodily-injury / attorney involvement — PASS, HISTORICAL
Primary source: Insurance Research Council study released 2026 using 2017–2022 claim data.
- Average BI claim payments rose from about $14,000 in 2017 to more than $20,000 in 2022, 7.8% annualized.
- Attorney representation across coverages rose from 40% to nearly 50%.
- Litigation rose from 10% to 18%.
Editorial constraint: these are historical study observations, not a statement that every current claim or jurisdiction follows the same trend.

## Qualitative claims cleared

### Risk pooling / geography — PASS
The script correctly states that a clean driving record is only one input to expected-loss pricing. NICB explicitly confirms location and local theft/accident risk are considered in underwriting. Keep state-law caveat for rating variables.

### Weather / catastrophe — PASS AS QUALITATIVE ONLY
The script makes no unsupported national dollar claim. Comprehensive vehicle losses can arise from hail, flooding, wildfire and severe storms without driver fault. Keep the wording at mechanism level unless a state/carrier-specific filing is shown on screen.

### State rate filings — PASS WITH CAVEAT
Keep: auto insurance is regulated at state level; filing/approval rules vary by jurisdiction; insurers may submit proposed changes and actuarial support; SERFF/public state systems can expose filings. Do not imply universal prior approval or identical disclosure rules.
